Everyone has that one liquor they can't shoot without gagging. The burn, the smell, the aftertaste that lingers for a full minute. Good news: taste is mostly mechanics, and you can hack the mechanics.

Here's what actually works, roughly from easiest to most effective.

## 1. Chill everything

Cold dulls flavor. A room-temperature shot of well tequila tastes like paint thinner; the same tequila straight from the freezer goes down clean. Keep the bottle in the freezer (liquor above 40 proof won't freeze) and pour into a chilled glass.

## 2. Breathe out, not in

Most of "taste" is smell. Exhale as you take the shot, swallow, then wait a beat before breathing in through your nose. Breathing in through your mouth mid-shot pulls the vapor straight across your palate — that's the burn you're trying to avoid.

## 3. Numb your tongue first

A bite of something cold — a spoonful of sorbet, a lick of an ice cube — briefly deadens the taste buds. Ten seconds is enough.

## 4. Pick a chaser that fights back

Match the chaser to the liquor:

- **Whiskey / bourbon** — ginger ale, cola, pickle juice
- **Tequila** — lime and salt, orange juice, grapefruit soda
- **Vodka** — cranberry, lemonade, plain water works fine
- **Rum** — pineapple or orange juice
- **Fireball and flavored whiskeys** — cold apple juice

The chaser should hit your tongue within about a second of swallowing. Any longer and the burn has already set in.

## 5. Don't sip it — commit

Tilt your head slightly forward, not back, and pour the shot past the front of your tongue toward the back of your mouth. Swallow immediately. Sipping keeps the liquid pooled on the most taste-sensitive part of your tongue.

## 6. Warm the glass in your hand (for sipping spirits)

This is the opposite of tip #1 and only applies to good whiskey or aged rum you actually want to taste a little. Skip it for shots you're trying not to taste.

## 7. Use a shot straw

This is the one that removes taste almost entirely. A shot straw like the **Take V2** holds your 1 oz shot sealed inside a straw. You drop the straw into your chaser, drink the chaser normally, and the shot releases *through* the chaser on its way down — so the alcohol never really lands on your tongue at all. No head tilt, no timing the chaser, no burn.

It's also the only method on this list that works for the stuff that's genuinely hard to drink: [wellness shots](/blog/how-to-take-wellness-shots-without-gagging), apple cider vinegar, sea moss, liquid supplements.

## The short version

If you just want the burn gone tonight: freezer-cold liquor, exhale through the shot, cold chaser ready in your hand. If you want to never taste a shot again, get a shot straw and stop fighting it.
